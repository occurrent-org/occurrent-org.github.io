## Contact & Support

Would you like to contribute to Occurrent? That's great! You should join the [mailing-list](https://groups.google.com/g/occurrent) or contribute on [github](https://github.com/johanhaleby/occurrent). The [mailing-list](https://groups.google.com/g/occurrent) can also be used for support and discussions.
